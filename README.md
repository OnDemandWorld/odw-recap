# Recap — Built

Recap turns meetings into structured action items, on-device. Its emotional hook: meeting recordings and transcripts never leave your machine — no cloud bot silently joining your calls, no transcripts sitting on a vendor's servers.

## Differentiator
Captures conversations, transcribes them locally (built on open transcription models), and produces clean summaries and action items that auto-sync into your tools. Real value is as a sovereign data source feeding the suite — meetings flow into Vault (knowledge) and Loop (workflows).

## Target
Privacy-conscious teams; suite integration makes it more than a commodity transcription tool.

## Monetization
Free core handles on-device transcription and notes; paid layer adds cross-module sync, retention and governance controls, and team features.

## Positioning
Bundle-first, not sold as a standalone transcription product.
